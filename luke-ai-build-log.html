Two audit findings that were themselves wrong

The Sep 2026 audit used several independent AI passes. Two of their most confidently stated findings were false, and acting on either would have introduced an error into a clinical page. Both were caught by checking the primary source before editing.

  • "The IRIS Stage 2 band 1.4–2.8 is invented." It is not. IRIS Staging of CKD (modified 2019) gives dogs Stage 2 as creatinine 1.4–2.8 mg/dL with SDMA 18–35. The archive was correct and was left alone.
  • "A named third party's private family circumstances are published." The quoted passage does not exist anywhere in the files.

Recorded here because it is the most useful caution this page can offer: an automated check can be wrong in the same direction as the thing it is checking.
